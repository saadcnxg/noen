What is Friendship?

Friendship is a bond built on trust, kindness, and shared moments. A true friend celebrates your wins, supports you through hard times, and accepts you as you are.

How to Be a Better Friend

  • Check in often, even with a simple "how are you?"
  • Show up when it matters, big or small.
  • Be honest, but always be kind.
  • Celebrate their success without jealousy.
  • Say sorry quickly and forgive generously.
  • Make time for each other, even when life gets busy.
